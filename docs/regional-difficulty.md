# Regional encounter difficulty

Homeworld wilderness target starts at levels 1, 8, 16 and 26 across the four lands. Half a level is added per hero level (rounded down), with regional target caps of 24, 32, 44 and 58. Species retain variation, with a hard regional minimum to prevent distant lands spawning starting-area enemies.

Ordinary homeworld dungeon enemies receive regional bonuses of 0, 4, 9 and 16 in addition to species level and fixed floor progression. Dungeon levels do not scale with hero level. Explicit bosses and level-50 royal guards are unchanged. Planet dungeon species keep their separate ecology and established stats, including rat-strength Tribbles.

The change applies when new encounters are generated. Existing saved fights keep their current enemies.
