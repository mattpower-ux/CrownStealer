# Release 0.9.50 — Five Cave Stories

Five formerly generic caves now contain persistent illustrated encounters while retaining their existing site IDs, coordinates, maps, and save compatibility.

- Moonpaw Cave: feed a moon-cursed werebear’s three dogs or provoke a fight.
- Fullbelly Burrow: bargain with Grub the food-hoarding kobold or rob his larder.
- Long-Ear Cave: rescue a rabbit from the dinner pot, leave, or fight the cook.
- Flowerhaven Hollow: free five missing Flowerhaven children from their captor.
- Timbertooth Lodge: help a beaver household repair its flooded underground home.

Every peaceful outcome, refusal, and hostile choice persists across save/load. Rewards are one-time. The five-panel original story atlas is bundled for offline play, and the former generic automatic guardians no longer interrupt these encounters.
