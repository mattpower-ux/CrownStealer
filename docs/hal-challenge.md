# Hal 2000: first to bust terminates

Hal replaces ordinary combat with Blackjack, Trivial Pursuit or Ship Engineering. Quiz questions and deliberately unanswerable offered choices are original game fiction. Every submitted quiz answer loses; ordinary attacks and items cannot bypass the challenge.

Blackjack has no gold wager. Player reaches 17 before standing; Hal draws to 17. Only a bust terminates a participant. If neither busts, another hand begins. Card/deck state and explosion phase persist in saves. A win runs 4.3 seconds of shaking, rising smoke and explosion before granting existing boss rewards once. Spoken lines use device speech synthesis where available, backed by electronic panic/explosion sounds and visible captions.

Loss deals exactly 500 damage, compresses the displayed room and shows the cubed adventurer ejected into space. Lethal damage uses normal defeat recovery. Both lethal and survivable losses leave the adventurer at the same alien planet landing site, avoiding an unintended route home. Hal remains undefeated and can be challenged again.

New built-in image generation prompts: (1) blue-green riveted alien supercomputer chamber with glowing red eye and hydraulic wall pistons, painterly comic fantasy sci-fi, no text; (2) cubed green-cloaked adventurer ejected through a ship airlock into space, humorous and non-gory, no UI.
