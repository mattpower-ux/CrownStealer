# Release 0.9.44 — November 17, 2026

Completed in this release:
- Tablet travel controls have no shared dark backing; removed the touch joystick. Direction arrows and hold-to-repeat remain.
- Character creation opens a full-body picker with five men and five women of varied ages, builds, hair and clothing. Selected bodies appear in play and the inventory; existing saved looks are preserved. Face changes retain the chosen body and appearance transformations retain their refusal rules.
- Set up Camp sleeps eight game hours and restores full health only when uninterrupted. Ambush chance is 25 percent. Guarded Sleep is a rare consumable scroll protecting the next camp, reducing the chance to 2.5 percent.
- Wilderness inactivity is checked after ten real seconds; its random encounter probability is half the corresponding normal travel probability. Checks pause in menus, hidden windows and existing encounters.
- Split Rock Cave uses gray rock and primitive predator paintings. Kerrbear is its peaceful level-15 bear, protecting three cubs. Feeding one meal grants one Lucky Beartooth Amulet; attack provokes combat. Wearing the amulet and calling his name three times summons him for one battle, once total.
- Kerrbear family illustration and ten optimized full-body assets.
- Mossy Shrine walls and vaulted roof visibly grow moss.

Validation: unit tests, browser review at tablet dimensions, character save/load and legacy appearance coverage, signed APK asset/signature verification, live website/APK checksum verification.
